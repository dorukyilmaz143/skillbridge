The gap we close

Experience requires a job. A job requires experience.

The problem

Talent is invisible on paper.

Most internships demand previous experience students don't have yet. Meanwhile, companies drown in identical CVs and can't tell who can actually do the work. Both sides lose.

Our solution

Let the work speak.

SkillBridge replaces guesswork with evidence. Students solve real business scenarios; companies evaluate real performance — not grades, not buzzwords, not templates.

How it works

From task to offer in five steps

  1. Companies post challenges

    Short, real-world business tasks and case studies — a few hours each.

  2. Students pick tasks

    Filtered by interest, field, and career goals.

  3. Solutions submitted

    Work is delivered right on the platform, portfolio-ready.

  4. Companies review

    Submissions are scored and top performers are flagged.

  5. Offers go out

    Standout students get interview invites or direct internship offers.
